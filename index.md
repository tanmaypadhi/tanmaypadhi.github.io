# Agentic Use Cases on CRM Platform of Salesforce

A curated collection of **Agentforce + Industry Cloud Applications + AI-driven Salesforce** architectures and real-world use cases I’ve designed and validated.

---

## What you’ll find here

- **Use cases** with clear problem → solution → outcomes
- **Reference architectures** and implementation notes
- **Prompting patterns** and guardrails (hallucination, privacy, security)
- **Testing strategy** (utterances, negative testing, data masking)

---

## Featured use cases

### ⚡ Energy & Utilities Cloud
- **Energy Advisor Agent** — Self-service insights, bill explanations, rate optimization, proactive recommendations  
- **Start/Stop Service Agent** — Conversational service requests with validation, identity checks, and backend updates  
- **Field Ops (SmartCamOps)** — AI-assisted mobile capture for compliance and faster resolution

### 🎬 Media Cloud
- **Compliance Agent** — Regulatory guidance + automated checks (GDPR/CCPA/DMCA/COPPA/FCC)
- **Evalyn: Customer Use Case Analyst** — AI-driven insights from instrumentation logs and customer journeys
- **Subscriber Incidents / QoE Agent** — Classify streaming issues, enrich cases, reduce churn

### ⚡ OmniStudio
- **OmniAgent** — Self-service insights, bill explanations, rate optimization, proactive recommendations

### 🧠 Support & Troubleshooting
- **SupportAI Investigator** — Multi-system investigation (cases, signals, org telemetry) with recommended resolutions

## How I structure each post

Each use case follows a consistent structure:

1. **Problem statement**
2. **Solution & agent behavior**
3. **Architecture & components**
4. **Example utterances**
5. **Security / privacy / guardrails**
6. **Testing strategy**
7. **Outcomes & learnings**
8. **Next enhancements**
